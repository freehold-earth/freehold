# Personal Context Infrastructure (PCI) - Executive Brief

## The Problem
**Big Tech has built a $2 trillion industry on harvesting your digital life.** Every click, purchase, and interaction trains AI models that will replace human workers while companies profit from selling your data. Privacy policies are theater - we need mathematical guarantees, not corporate promises.

## The Solution
**Personal Context Infrastructure (PCI)** - A four-layer sovereign stack that keeps your data local, proves facts without revealing details, and enforces privacy through cryptographic law.

### The Architecture (Simple Version)
1. **Your Data Vault** - Encrypted, synced across YOUR devices
2. **Your AI Assistant** - Runs locally, thinks using YOUR data
3. **Your Privacy Rules** - Smart contracts enforce your S-PAL (Sovereign Privacy & Access Language)
4. **Your Proof System** - Verify facts without exposing information
5. **Trust Registry** - Directory of approved verification providers (for businesses)

## Why Community Cloud Changes Everything

**The Innovation:** Between "run your own server" (too hard) and "use Big Tech" (defeats purpose) lies **Community Cloud** - local organizations running sovereignty infrastructure for their members.

### Already Happening
- **El Servidor del Barri** (Barcelona) - Neighborhoods running email, cloud storage
- **Community Box** (UK) - Rural communities with local cloud services
- **Guifi.net** - 39,000+ nodes proving community networks work
